BUILT WITH PURPOSE

Smart outside. Structured inside.

Smart E-Commerce Recommendation System · DBMS Cornerstone Project

01 / THE DATABASE

Eight connected tables.

App_User, Category, Product, Cart, Orders, Order_Item, Product_Interaction and Review organize customers, catalogue, purchases and feedback.

02 / NORMALISATION

Designed through 3NF.

Atomic order lines remove repeating groups. Separating orders and products removes partial dependencies. Customer and category details live in their own tables to remove transitive dependencies.

03 / RECOMMENDATIONS

Rules, not black boxes.

SQL combines average ratings with interaction counts, excluding products a customer already interacted with. Ratings and counts are aggregated separately to avoid join multiplication. Category history explains relevant matches.

04 / CONSISTENCY

One reliable transaction.

Checkout calculates prices on the server, validates stock, preserves the price charged and saves Orders, Order_Item and PURCHASE interactions together. The browser bag is not a new database table.

Presentation-only local application. No payment processing, production login or shipping integration. Existing records and database schema are preserved.
